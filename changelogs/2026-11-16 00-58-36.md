# Move the master meter beneath the fader and split it into stereo rows

The Master Gain meter now has two labelled horizontal rows, one for the left output and one for the right. Each row has eight LEDs with a diameter reduced from 11 px to 7 px, and both rows sit at the bottom of the slider. The processor measures and holds each channel independently. These readings remain transient display data.

Checks: the production UI and Release VST3 builds, both native suites and pluginval strictness 5 passed. Left-only and right-only stereo sample tests confirm that each active channel clips while the other stays dark; the left indication decays after silence. A headless preview confirmed the rows' bottom placement. The prior installed VST3 was backed up, and the new build was installed while Cubase was closed.
