# Add display fonts, master meter and value arcs

Embedded the supplied Honk and Pixelify Sans fonts in the VST3 editor. Honk styles the HYPERREAL logo; Pixelify Sans styles the selected pad/MIDI label, status and sample title. Added eight LEDs beside Master Gain to show post-gain output level, with four green, two amber and two red. The top red LED holds a clip indication briefly after the output reaches 0 dBFS. Corrected rotary highlight arcs so they end at the indicator.
