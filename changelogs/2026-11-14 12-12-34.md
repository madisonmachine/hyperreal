# Phase 3 pad operations

14 November 2026

Changes: added pad copy, paste, swap and clear for sample content and level, mode, choke, mute and solo settings. Paste is bounded by the 512 MB active kit limit. Swap keeps MIDI notes attached to grid slots. Paste, swap and clear invalidate affected pending loads; already playing voices keep their sample. The editor offers a two-step confirmation before destructive operations. Clipboard data is temporary and is not part of project state.

Checks: React/TypeScript UI build, Release VST3 and standalone build, engine and processor smoke tests, and pluginval strictness 5 passed. Tests cover copy/paste, swapped MIDI mapping, settings transfer, clear defaults and preservation of a voice already playing after clear.
