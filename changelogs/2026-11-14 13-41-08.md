# Phase 3 Cubase feedback and pad interaction fix

14 November 2026

Cubase findings: the custom 16-pad v0.3.0 editor showed VOICES and VELOCITY; LOAD TO PAD 01 and its AUDITION button worked promptly; copy/paste buttons worked; saved pads reopened READY and audible. Dragging test WAV files onto pads left them EMPTY. Pad audition required a double-click and felt delayed. The owner requested loading and pad edits from right-click menus and audition from a single pad click.

Changes: pad single-click now sends audition immediately. Right-click opens pad-specific LOAD SAMPLE, COPY, PASTE, SWAP and CLEAR actions; paste, swap and clear retain confirmation. The old load, audition and pad-operation buttons were removed. WebView file drops now transfer bounded chunks through the native bridge into a memory-backed worker import, avoiding the OS drag path that failed in Cubase. Progress and errors are shown in the editor.

Checks: TypeScript/Vite production build, Release VST3/standalone build, native engine and processor tests, and pluginval strictness 5 passed. A native test loads a memory-backed WAV into a pad and plays it via MIDI. Cubase retesting of drop delivery, single-click audition and right-click actions remains open.
