# Phase 3 mute and multi-solo

14 November 2026

Checks: React/TypeScript production build, Release VST3 and standalone build, engine and processor smoke tests, and pluginval strictness 5 passed. Tests cover muted triggers, multiple simultaneous solos, active-voice fade, command validation, current-state recall and HRP4 migration.
