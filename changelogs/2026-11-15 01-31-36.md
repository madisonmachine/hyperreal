# Pad-click MIDI output

Enabled HYPERREAL's VST3 MIDI output. Clicking a loaded pad still auditions it and now emits its mapped MIDI note (36–51, channel 1) with a 100 ms gate. The processor consumes keyboard MIDI without echoing it to the new output. Added a regression for the click event and updated the Phase 4 host-check list.

Cubase records MIDI at a track's input, before the instrument. To capture clicks, route HYPERREAL's MIDI output into a separate record-enabled MIDI track; the same instrument track cannot capture its own plug-in output. Host routing remains unverified until the updated bundle is installed and tested in Cubase.

Checks: Release VST3 build, both native CTest suites and pluginval strictness 5 passed.
