# Separate pad settings and add per-pad M/S controls

Mode, Choke Group, Voices and Velocity now sit in their own black LED-style screen, clear of the editing tabs. The main editing display has a uniform border and corrected tab-panel join. Each pad has small Mute and Solo buttons at its top-right corner; their active states light independently. The former Mute and Solo checkboxes were removed from the settings strip.

Checks: the production UI and Release VST3 builds and pluginval strictness 5 passed. Headless 900 px and 560 px previews confirmed the screen separation, border alignment, dropdown placement, and readable pad labels beside the M/S buttons. The previous installed VST3 was backed up, and the new build was installed while Cubase was closed. Its installed binary SHA-256 matches the Release build (`E2C3073B054B2E14C4C7BC31431295A2FA140780119DB2CE7CB852D989ACA07F`).
